Lingxuan Wu

Joint PhD student in Mathematics

I am a joint PhD student at Central South University and Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, co-supervised by Prof. Yong Jiao and Prof. Elba García-Failde.

My research focuses on free probability, random matrix theory, and topological recursion, especially higher-order free probability, non-Hermitian random matrices, and the links between free probability and topological recursion.

Research Interests

  • Higher-order free probability and fluctuations of random matrices
  • Non-Hermitian random matrices, especially Gaussian elliptic matrices
  • Topological recursion, loop equations, and map enumeration
  • Connections between free probability and topological recursion
