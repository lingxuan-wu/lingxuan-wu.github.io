Lingxuan Wu

Joint PhD student in Mathematics

I am a joint PhD student in mathematics at Central South University and Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, supervised by Professor Yong Jiao and Professor Elba García-Failde.

My research interests include higher-order free probability, non-Hermitian random matrices, and topological recursion. I am especially interested in the connections between free probability and topological recursion.
