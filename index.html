I am a PhD student in mathematics at Central South University. My research lies in free probability, random matrix theory, and topological recursion.

I am currently interested in second-order freeness, fluctuations of random matrices, Gaussian elliptic matrices, second-order Cauchy transforms, and the connections between random matrices, map enumeration, and topological recursion.

Research Interests

  • Free probability and random matrix theory
  • Second-order freeness and global fluctuations of random matrices
  • Gaussian elliptic matrices and elliptic Wishart-type models
  • Second-order Cauchy transforms and analytic second-order distributions
  • Topological recursion, loop equations, and map enumeration
